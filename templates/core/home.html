What it does

Built for systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and coordinated distributed experiments in ecology, biology, and medicine.

Task & verification

Assign, complete, and peer-verify tasks. Each closed task becomes a permanent, attributable contribution record.

Contribution ledger

Cross-project contribution history, mapped toward CRediT taxonomy for fair, evidence-based authorship conversations.

Global, ORCID-linked

Free for external collaborators. ORCID identity links keep researcher profiles portable across institutions.
