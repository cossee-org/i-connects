How completion works

  1. 1. Done. Each assignee ticks Done when their part is finished; a Lead can tick it for anyone. Untick to undo.
  2. 2. Reviewed. On a task with reviewers, a reviewer or a Lead ticks Reviewed once a part is done. Whoever reviewed it, or a Lead, can untick it. Undoing a Done mark clears its review.
  3. 3. Verified. A Lead ticks Verified to confirm a member's contribution, which is what credits them for it. Leads can verify one part at a time, or use Verify all marked done or Verify all marked reviewed. Undoing a Done mark clears its verification.
  4. 4. Completed. A Lead marks the whole task completed with Mark completed at the top. Assignees can still tick their own Done and Leads can still verify after that, and reviewers can review parts marked done after completion. Parts already marked when the task was completed stay as they were: a review given then is locked, and a part left unreviewed can no longer be reviewed. The Done marks a Lead sets for others are locked too; a Lead marks the task not complete to change any of these. A review given after completion can still be unticked, and an assignee can still untick their own Done, which clears its review and verification.

Each header sorts the rows in the browser, ascending first; clicking the active header flips the direction. Assignee sorts by name; Done puts parts done first, earliest first, then the rest; Reviewed puts parts reviewed first, earliest first, then those awaiting review, then the rest.
