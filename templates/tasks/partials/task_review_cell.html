{% comment %} The Reviewed cell of one completion-matrix row: the verdict, who gave it, when — and the reviewer's own buttons. Expects ``s`` (one entry of views._task_status_context's assignee_states) and ``task``. The buttons swap #task-status-block, the same target the assignee's own mark uses, so one round trip re-renders the whole matrix and the pane's top bar with it. Nothing here is stored per viewer: ``s.can_review`` is recomputed from the task's live reviewer set on every render, so a reviewer added or removed gains or loses these buttons immediately. ``s.can_unreview`` is the narrower of the two, and why the two buttons read different flags: a reviewer may withdraw the verdict they gave and no one else's. A Lead can clear any of them. Marking the task completed retires these buttons, exactly as it retires the assignee's own mark: a completed task's marks and reviews are frozen until a Lead marks it not complete again. The name and the awaiting note are text-xs, matching the date the cell prints beside them: this column can carry a check, a date, a name and a button at once, and at the table's text-sm that wraps in a narrow pane. {% endcomment %}
