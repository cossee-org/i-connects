{% comment %} The Reviewed cell of one completion-matrix row: the verdict, who gave it, when — and the reviewer's own buttons. Expects ``s`` (one entry of views._task_status_context's assignee_states) and ``task``. The buttons swap #task-status-block, the same target the assignee's own mark uses, so one round trip re-renders the whole matrix and the pane's top bar with it. Nothing here is stored per viewer: ``s.can_review`` is recomputed from the task's live reviewer set on every render, so a reviewer added or removed gains or loses these buttons immediately — and it is false on the viewer's own row, since signing off your own submission is not a peer review. Closing the task does not retire these buttons the way it retires the assignee's own mark: a Lead may well close first and have the reviewers sign off after. {% endcomment %}
